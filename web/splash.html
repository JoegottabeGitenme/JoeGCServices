                    Weather for your AI assistant
                


                

                    

                        Ask your AI assistant “what’s it like out there today?”
                        and get a short, grounded read built on NOAA and NASA data — with an old weather
                        saying when one genuinely fits, and the science behind why.
                    

                    

                        FolkWeather’s MCP server lets an assistant like Claude or ChatGPT fetch the real forecast,
                        observations and alerts for your town, plus any weather saying that fits the day. The assistant
                        writes the words; the numbers and the sources come from us.
                    

                    

                        Built on HRRR, NBM and GFS model data, airport METAR observations, MRMS radar rainfall,
                        official NWS alerts, and sun and moon tables. If the best model is late, the next one answers
                        and says so.

What a saying looks like

                        

                            

                                
Lore entry · evening_outlook

                                Illustration, not today’s weather
                            

                            
Clear moon, frost soon.

                            

                                Attested
                                Merit: strong
                                Holds today: yes
                            

                            

                                
Why today

                                
Clear skies, 1.2 m/s winds overnight, dewpoint −3 °C at dusk (HRRR); moon 78% lit.

                                
The physics

                                
On a clear, calm night the ground radiates heat straight to space with no cloud to trap it. If the air is dry, the surface cools below the dewpoint and frost forms before dawn.

                                
Printed source

                                
Clear moon, / Frost soon. — Abercromby & Marriott, 1882, via Dunwoody, Weather Proverbs (U.S. Signal Service, 1883).

                                
It predicts

                                
Frost by dawn: a low at or below 0 °C within the next 12 hours.

                            

                        

                    


                    

                        
How the lore stays honest

                        
    
                            
  • Tied to measurements. Every saying has a measurable precondition, checked against today’s data before it is offered.
    • 
                            
  • Printed word for word. 14 of the 15 sayings are attested, with the exact printed wording, from public-domain collections: Dunwoody’s Weather Proverbs (1883), FitzRoy (1859), Wright (1913), and Abercromby & Marriott (1882).
    • 
                            
  • Ours are labeled ours. House sayings are marked as FolkWeather’s own and never passed off as old wisdom.
    • 
                            
  • Color, never authority. A saying can’t change a number or hide an alert.
    • 
                            
  • Quiet days stay quiet. If nothing fits, it says nothing rather than inventing a saying.
    • 
                            
  • It tells you when they disagree. Each saying reports whether it agrees with today’s forecast.
    • 
                        

                    

                


                
Private beta — sayings are self-reviewed; detailed models cover the continental US.

                

                    Alaska, Hawaii, Puerto Rico and Guam get a coarser read; anywhere else uses the global model only,
                    and the answer says so. Name a US city or ZIP code, or give latitude and longitude.

                📊
                Available Data Sources
            

            

                

                    

                        🌎
                        GFS - Global Forecast
                    

                    

                        Global Forecast System with 0.25 degree resolution. Temperature, wind, humidity, precipitation, and more with forecasts out to 384 hours.
                    

                

                

                    

                        🌦️
                        HRRR - High Resolution
                    

                    

                        High-Resolution Rapid Refresh with 3km resolution and hourly updates. Best for short-term forecasts over CONUS. Reflectivity and precipitation type also come every 15 minutes.
                    

                

                

                    

                        🛰️
                        MRMS Radar
                    

                    

                        Multi-Radar Multi-Sensor composite with 2-minute updates. Real-time precipitation and reflectivity over CONUS.
                    

                

                

                    

                        📡
                        GOES-18 Satellite
                    

                    

                        GOES West geostationary satellite. CONUS imagery every 5 minutes: visible (C02) and clean infrared (C13).
                    

                

                

                    

                        🌞
                        GOES-19 Satellite
                    

                    

                        GOES East geostationary satellite. CONUS imagery every 5 minutes: visible (C02) and clean infrared (C13).
                    

                

                

                    

                        🌡️
                        NBM - National Blend
                    

                    

                        National Blend of Models with 2.5km resolution. Statistically post-processed blend of multiple models for optimal forecast accuracy. CONUS, Alaska, Hawaii, Puerto Rico, and Guam.
                    

                

                

                    

                        📅
                        NDFD - National Digital Forecast
                    

                    

                        The National Weather Service’s own forecast grids for the contiguous US. Temperature, dew point, humidity, wind, sky cover, and chance of precipitation.
                    

                

                

                    

                        🌊
                        GFS-Wave (WAVEWATCH III)
                    

                    

                        Global wave forecasts at 0.25° resolution. Significant wave height, period, direction, wind waves, and surface wind. Updated every 6 hours with 3-hourly forecasts out to 384 hours (16 days).
                    

                

                

                    

                        🌍
                        SST & Sea Ice
                    

                    

                        GFS sea surface temperature, sea ice concentration, thickness, and temperature. Global coverage at 0.25° resolution with 6-hourly updates.
                    

                

                

                    

                        🌱
                        GLDAS-2.1 Noah Land Surface
                    

                    

                        NASA GLDAS-2.1 Noah land surface model, global at 0.25° and 3-hourly. Soil moisture and temperature, snow, evapotranspiration, surface fluxes, and forcing. Runs about a month behind real time.
                    

                

                

                    

                        ⚓
                        NDBC Buoys
                    

                    

                        Real-time surface observations from ~860 NDBC buoys and coastal stations. Wind, pressure, air/water temperature, wave height, wave period, and tide levels.
                    

                

                

                    

                        🌏
                        DART Tsunami Buoys
                    

                    

                        Deep-ocean water column height from ~40 DART tsunami detection buoys. Standard 15-minute reporting with 1-minute and 15-second event modes during seismic activity.
                    

                

                

                    

                        ✈️
                        METAR & TAF
                    

                    

                        Airport weather reports and forecasts. METAR observations of temperature, wind, visibility, pressure, and flight category, and 24-30 hour TAF forecasts.
                    

                

                

                    

                        ⚡
                        GLM Lightning
                    

                    

                        Lightning flashes seen by the GOES Geostationary Lightning Mapper over CONUS for the last 24 hours, one point per flash with its time and energy, about a minute behind real time.
                    

                

                

                    

                        🌪️
                        Storm Reports
                    

                    

                        NOAA Storm Events since 1995: hail size, thunderstorm wind speed, and tornado tracks with their EF rating.
                    

                

                

                    

                        🥾
                        Trails
                    

                    

                        OpenStreetMap trails, tracks, and bridleways across Colorado, with soil saturation for the Front Range foothills.
                    

                

                

                    

                        🌙
                        Astronomy
                    

                    

                        Sunrise, sunset, moonrise, moonset, moon phase, and the sun’s position for any place and time, computed on request.
                    

                

                

                    

                        ⚠️
                        NWS Alerts
                    

                    

                        Every watch, warning and advisory the National Weather Service has in force, checked every minute and kept for 7 days. Each comes with its outline, the NWS map colour and, where NWS labels its text, a short what/where/when summary.
