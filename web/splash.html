💬 Weather for your AI assistant

Ask your AI assistant “what’s it like out there today?” and get a short, grounded read built on NOAA and NASA data — with an old weather saying when one genuinely fits, and the science behind why.

FolkWeather’s MCP server lets an assistant like Claude or ChatGPT fetch the real forecast, observations and alerts for your town, plus any weather saying that fits the day. The assistant writes the words; the numbers and the sources come from us.

Built on HRRR, NBM and GFS model data, airport METAR observations, MRMS radar rainfall, official NWS alerts, and sun and moon tables. If the best model is late, the next one answers and says so.

Server URL
https://folkweather.com/mcp
Remote MCP over streamable HTTP · no sign-in · free

Connect it

💬 Claude
Add to Claude

Opens Claude with FolkWeather filled in; check the details and choose Add. Or by hand:

  1. Open Settings, then Connectors.
  2. Choose Add custom connector.
  3. Paste https://folkweather.com/mcp and save.
💻 Claude Code

Run this once in your terminal:

claude mcp add --transport http folkweather https://folkweather.com/mcp
🧩 ChatGPT

Custom connectors need a paid plan and are added in ChatGPT on the web, not the phone app: turn on developer mode in Settings, then add a connector (some versions call it an app) with the URL above. No sign-in is needed. Menu names change between releases.

🔗 Any MCP client

Add https://folkweather.com/mcp as a remote server over streamable HTTP. There is no authentication and no API key.

On your phone, or sending it to a friend? folkweather.com/connect has one-tap setup and steps for each assistant. Documentation · Privacy

What it can do

🌞 Daily read weather + lore
  • morning_almanac The day ahead: high and low, sky, rain or snow and when, wind, pressure trend, yesterday’s actuals, sun and moon, NWS alerts, and up to three sayings that fit.
  • evening_outlook Tonight and tomorrow, plus the evening signs: red sky, a ring around the moon, dew and frost.
  • lore_lookup Where a saying comes from: the mechanism, the printed source, and its provenance.
📊 Data numbers only
  • hourly_forecast Hour-by-hour numbers for up to 48 hours.
  • current_conditions The nearest airport observation (with the raw METAR), the current model hour, and any alerts.
  • point_query Advanced: any model collection and parameter at a point.

Also included: reference pages your assistant can read (about, the writing style guide, the lore index, the dataset catalog) and a daily_read prompt that writes the morning brief.

What a saying looks like

Lore entry · evening_outlook
Illustration, not today’s weather

Clear moon, frost soon.

Attested Merit: strong Holds today: yes
Why today
Clear skies, 1.2 m/s winds overnight, dewpoint −3 °C at dusk (HRRR); moon 78% lit.
The physics
On a clear, calm night the ground radiates heat straight to space with no cloud to trap it. If the air is dry, the surface cools below the dewpoint and frost forms before dawn.
Printed source
Clear moon, / Frost soon. — Abercromby & Marriott, 1882, via Dunwoody, Weather Proverbs (U.S. Signal Service, 1883).
It predicts
Frost by dawn: a low at or below 0 °C within the next 12 hours.

How the lore stays honest

  • Tied to measurements. Every saying has a measurable precondition, checked against today’s data before it is offered.
  • Printed word for word. 14 of the 15 sayings are attested, with the exact printed wording, from public-domain collections: Dunwoody’s Weather Proverbs (1883), FitzRoy (1859), Wright (1913), and Abercromby & Marriott (1882).
  • Ours are labeled ours. House sayings are marked as FolkWeather’s own and never passed off as old wisdom.
  • Color, never authority. A saying can’t change a number or hide an alert.
  • Quiet days stay quiet. If nothing fits, it says nothing rather than inventing a saying.
  • It tells you when they disagree. Each saying reports whether it agrees with today’s forecast.

Private beta — sayings are self-reviewed; detailed models cover the continental US.

Alaska, Hawaii, Puerto Rico and Guam get a coarser read; anywhere else uses the global model only, and the answer says so. Name a US city or ZIP code, or give latitude and longitude.

                📊
                Available Data Sources
            

            

                

                    

                        🌎
                        GFS - Global Forecast
                    

                    

                        Global Forecast System with 0.25 degree resolution. Temperature, wind, humidity, precipitation, and more with forecasts out to 384 hours.
                    

                

                

                    

                        🌦
                        HRRR - High Resolution
                    

                    

                        High-Resolution Rapid Refresh with 3km resolution and hourly updates. Best for short-term forecasts over CONUS.
                    

                

                

                    

                        🛰
                        MRMS Radar
                    

                    

                        Multi-Radar Multi-Sensor composite with 2-minute updates. Real-time precipitation and reflectivity over CONUS.
                    

                

                

                    

                        📡
                        GOES-18 Satellite
                    

                    

                        GOES West geostationary satellite. CONUS imagery every 5 minutes: visible (C02) and clean infrared (C13).
                    

                

                

                    

                        🌞
                        GOES-19 Satellite
                    

                    

                        GOES East geostationary satellite. CONUS imagery every 5 minutes: visible (C02) and clean infrared (C13).
                    

                

                

                    

                        🌡
                        NBM - National Blend
                    

                    

                        National Blend of Models with 2.5km resolution. Statistically post-processed blend of multiple models for optimal forecast accuracy. CONUS, Alaska, Hawaii, Puerto Rico, and Guam.
                    

                

                

                    

                        🌊
                        GFS-Wave (WAVEWATCH III)
                    

                    

                        Global wave forecasts at 0.25° resolution. Significant wave height, period, direction, wind waves, and surface wind. Updated every 6 hours with forecasts to 120 hours.
                    

                

                

                    

                        🌍
                        SST & Sea Ice
                    

                    

                        GFS sea surface temperature, sea ice concentration, thickness, and temperature. Global coverage at 0.25° resolution with 6-hourly updates.
                    

                

                

                    

                        🌱
                        NLDAS-2 Noah Land Surface
                    

                    

                        NASA NLDAS-2 Noah land surface model at 0.125° hourly resolution. 37 variables including soil moisture, soil temperature, snow, radiation, and evapotranspiration over CONUS.
                    

                

                

                    

                        ☁
                        NLDAS-2 Forcing
                    

                    

                        NASA NLDAS-2 atmospheric forcing data at 0.125° hourly resolution. Air temperature, precipitation, humidity, pressure, wind, and radiation over CONUS.
                    

                

                

                    

                        ⚓
                        NDBC Buoys
                    

                    

                        Real-time surface observations from ~749 NDBC buoys and coastal stations. Wind, pressure, air/water temperature, wave height, wave period, and tide levels.
                    

                

                

                    

                        🌏
                        DART Tsunami Buoys
                    

                    

                        Deep-ocean water column height from ~54 DART tsunami detection buoys. Standard 15-minute reporting with 1-minute and 15-second event modes during seismic activity.
